The site is up. Posts are Markdown files in `_posts/`, named
`YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md`, and they get URLs like `/posts/2026/09/hello/`.

Front matter needs a `title`; `description` and `tags` are optional. The layout
is applied automatically, so a post is just:

```markdown
---
title: Setting up
tags: [meta]
---

Body text here.
```

Everything in `src/` is rebuilt by esbuild, so adding a Web Component to a post
is a matter of dropping the tag into the Markdown and registering the element in
`src/js/main.js`.
